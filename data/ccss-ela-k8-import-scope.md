# Common Core ELA K–8 import scope

Framework: Common Core State Standards (CCSS), national multi-state framework.
Jurisdiction key: National.
Subject: English Language Arts & Literacy.

## Authoritative source
Use the official ADA-compliant standards document as the primary transcription and verification source:
https://corestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ADA-Compliant-ELA-Standards.pdf

Official landing page:
https://corestandards.org/english-language-arts-standards/

The official document distinguishes the comprehensive K–5 ELA section, the 6–12 ELA section, and the 6–12 literacy section for history/social studies, science, and technical subjects. K–8 grade-specific standards are included. The supplemental 6–8 literacy standards must be stored as a distinct strand/group and not presented as ordinary ELA-only standards.

## K–8 strands to import
- RL — Reading: Literature (K–8)
- RI — Reading: Informational Text (K–8)
- RF — Reading: Foundational Skills (K–5 only)
- W — Writing (K–8)
- SL — Speaking and Listening (K–8)
- L — Language (K–8)
- RH — Reading: History/Social Studies (6–8 supplemental literacy)
- RST — Reading: Science and Technical Subjects (6–8 supplemental literacy)
- WHST — Writing: History/Social Studies, Science, and Technical Subjects (6–8 supplemental literacy)

## Import rules
1. Preserve the nine campaign-era curated records; do not replace their IDs, wording, or curated flag.
2. Import grade-specific standards and their lettered subparts as separate searchable records when the official source assigns them distinct codes.
3. Preserve official codes and wording verbatim. Do not invent wording for entries marked “begins in grade …” or “not applicable”; these are not grade-specific learning expectations and should not be loaded as ordinary standards.
4. Keep teacher-friendly title, summary, and keywords separate from exact official wording.
5. Every imported record must include its official source URL and source-check date.
6. Use framework=CCSS and jurisdiction=National. Keep any future Arizona standards in a separate framework/jurisdiction namespace.
7. Validate imported record counts and sample codes by grade/strand against the official PDF before running the import against the live D1 database.
