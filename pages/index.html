A LITTLE GUIDANCE

From a standard
to your classroom.

1. Choose your starting point

The library includes RF.3.3c, RF.4.3a, RF.5.3a, RI.3.2–RI.5.2, and W.3.1–W.5.1. The CLOVER campaign example uses Grade 4, RF.4.3a.

For another subject, grade, or framework, use “Other standard: prompt.” You receive a planning prompt, not an automatically generated lesson. The generator does not guess official wording from a code.

2. Generate, then adapt

Choose your time and learner supports. Generate a plan, student practice, and teacher key. Use “Edit text” to adjust any section, then copy, download, print, or save your edited version.

For CLOVER, the six-pattern option is a review. Teach a single pattern first if it is new to your students. A short lesson addresses part of a broad standard, not complete mastery.

3. Take the lesson with you

Copy the lesson into Google Docs or Word, download a plain-text version, or choose “Print / PDF” and select “Save as PDF” in your browser. All three lesson tabs are included.

“Save lesson” keeps up to 30 lessons on this device. Saved lessons do not sync between devices. Download a copy before clearing browser data.

What makes it work?

This self-contained file assembles the nine curated teaching routines on your device. It makes no AI, Netlify, Airtable, or email-service requests. Other standards create a prompt only.

Saved plans stay in your browser. Download your lessons to keep permanent copies. You can open this file without ChatGPT, a Claude account, or an internet connection. Official source links open online only if you choose them.
